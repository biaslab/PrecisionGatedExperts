Key Insight

Quality depends only on σ, not on μ. The log-mean just shifts both distributions on log-scale.

α* ≈ 1/σ²    β* = exp(ψ(α*) − μ)

The Gamma's exponential tail limits the fit: when σ is large, the LogNormal's heavier tail can't be captured.

σ ≤ 0.3 → KL < 0.01 (excellent)
σ ≈ 0.6 → KL ≈ 0.03 (good)
σ > 1.0 → poor (tail mismatch)
